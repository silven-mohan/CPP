# Important Notes:


## Initializations:

1\. Copy Initialization: `int a = 10;` – Uses the assignment operator (`=`) to initialize a variable.

2\. Direct Initialization: `int a(10);` – Uses parentheses (`()`) to directly initialize a variable.

3\. Direct-list Initialization: `int a{10};` – Uses curly braces (`{}`) to initialize a variable and prevents narrowing conversions.
